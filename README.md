## 📌 Project Overview

Democracy isn't grinding down solely due to voter apathy—it is grinding down because everyday participation has been allowed to become an un-guided second job. While most civic tech focuses on downstream tools (voter registration apps, turnout dashboards), it feeds into an already burnt-out user base.

Synthesizing **48 raw signals of friction** from constituents, local volunteers, and grassroots organizers, our research triangulates the **"Extraction Trap"**: institutions routinely collect sentiment without providing visible impact or structural support.

### Methodology: Frame Creation & Innovation
Following **Kees Dorst's Frame Creation Framework**, this research pod focuses on **problem saturation before solution engineering**:
* **Frame Innovation:** Uncovering the underlying paradoxes and systemic causes of civic fatigue.
* **Reframing:** Shifting the paradigm from "optimizing civic noise" to unlocking genuine human agency.
* **Systemic Interventions:** Designing sustainable scaffolds that lower participation barriers at the root.
